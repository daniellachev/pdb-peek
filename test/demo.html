How hemoglobin carries oxygen

Lecture notes, week 4

Hemoglobin is a tetramer of two alpha and two beta chains, each holding a heme group. The oxygenated form of human hemoglobin was solved by X-ray crystallography at 2.1 Å (PDB: 1HHO), and the deoxygenated form is entry 4HHB.

When oxygen binds, the tetramer shifts from the T state to the R state. Compare the spike trimers in PDB IDs 6VXX, 6VYB and 6VSB for another example of a protein that changes shape.

PDB Peek

Privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

What PDB Peek collects

Nothing. PDB Peek has no analytics, no advertising, no account and no server of its own. It does not store or send the pages you visit, your browsing history or anything you type, apart from the search text described below.

Requests it makes

When you hover or open a PDB ID, PDB Peek requests that entry's metadata from data.rcsb.org and its coordinates from models.rcsb.org. When you search by name in the popup, it sends your search text to search.rcsb.org. These requests go directly from your browser to the RCSB Protein Data Bank, whose own policies apply.

How it reads pages

On the sites where it runs, PDB Peek looks for PDB IDs in the page text and in links, then marks them in place. This happens inside your browser. The page content is never sent anywhere.

Settings

Your two settings (highlighting on or off; running on every website or not) are kept in Chrome's sync storage, which Chrome manages for your Google account.
